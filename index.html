Research

🤖

Language models as coding agents

Methods that let language models reason over code, interact with external tools, and use structured context to solve complex programming tasks — improving the reliability, controllability, and practical usefulness of code-generating agents in real development settings.

  • Prompt & context design
  • Tool-augmented generation
  • Code understanding & editing
  • Agent evaluation
🧠

General LLM research

LLM-based methods and analyses that explain how language models behave, and that strengthen their capabilities through both training and inference-time techniques.

  • Training
  • Inference-time methods
  • Interpretability & analysis

ACL Rolling Review (ARR) is the centralized peer-review system of the Association for Computational Linguistics, serving venues such as ACL, EMNLP, and NAACL.
Among the **10,636 reviewers** of the May 2026 cycle (source: [An explanatory letter on the ARR May 2026 cycle](https://aclrollingreview.org/may26-cycle-letter)), only **231 (2.17%)** were selected as Outstanding Reviewers for the quality of their reviews.

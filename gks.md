For prospective international applicants who wish to join our laboratory through the GKS scholarship program, please note that I only accept students who have **previously co-authored and published an SCI(E)-indexed journal paper with me**.

In addition, applicants are strongly encouraged to contact me well in advance of the official application period. In your initial contact email, you should clearly explain:

- the research topic you would like to pursue in our lab,
- the specific ideas or hypotheses you want to investigate, and
- how you plan to design and carry out the experiments.

If you have already conducted relevant experiments, please include your results, figures, or analysis. If possible, attach a draft manuscript, research proposal, or preliminary write-up so that I can evaluate your readiness and research direction more effectively.

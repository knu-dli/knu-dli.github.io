## Eligibility

For prospective international applicants who wish to join our laboratory through the GKS scholarship program, I only consider applicants who meet **all** of the following criteria:

- **Academic record:** a cumulative GPA (CGPA) of **3.9 or higher** (on a 4.0 scale).
- **First-author NLP publication:** you have published, as the **first author**, a paper on an NLP-related topic in an NLP-related journal or conference.

Applications that do not meet these criteria will not be considered.

## How to Contact Me

Applicants are strongly encouraged to contact me well in advance of the official application period. In your initial contact email, you should clearly explain:

- the research topic you would like to pursue in our lab,
- the specific ideas or hypotheses you want to investigate, and
- how you plan to design and carry out the experiments.

Please also include your CGPA and the full reference of your first-author NLP publication. If you have already conducted relevant experiments, please include your results, figures, or analysis. If possible, attach a draft manuscript, research proposal, or preliminary write-up so that I can evaluate your readiness and research direction more effectively.

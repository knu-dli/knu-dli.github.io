bio: >-
  I am an assistant professor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Kyungpook National
  University (KNU), and a faculty member of the Data & Language Intelligence Lab. My research focuses on
  language models for code: how they reason over code, use tools and repository-level context,
  and work as reliable coding agents, along with how to evaluate them.


  I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from POSTECH, advised by
  [Prof. Hwanjo Yu](https://sites.google.com/view/postechdi/member/faculty). Before joining KNU,
  I was a postdoctoral researcher at the POSTECH Data Intelligence Lab and a machine learning
  researcher at ScatterLab.
